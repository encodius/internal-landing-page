02Engagement

Three ways to work with us

01

Delivery team

We take a defined scope end to end — analysis, build, testing, release — and report against agreed milestones. Suited to a new system or a self-contained module.

02

Embedded engineers

Our people join your team, your process and your ceremonies, reporting to your leads. Suited to sustained capacity you cannot hire for quickly.

03

Specialist role

One person for one gap — a platform engineer, a security reviewer, a mobile lead — for a fixed period. Suited to unblocking a delivery already under way.

03How we work
STEP 1

A conversation

One call with the people who will actually do the work, not an account manager.

STEP 2

A written proposal

Scope, roles, assumptions and what we think the risks are, in plain language.

STEP 3

A named team

You see CVs and meet the engineers before they start. No substitutions without agreement.

STEP 4

Work in the open

Your repositories, your board, your review process. Progress is visible without a status meeting.

What we will tell you no to

Work we cannot staff with people we have already worked with. We would rather decline than assemble strangers around your project.

Fixed prices on scopes nobody has written down yet. We will do the analysis first, as a small paid piece, and quote after it.

Deadlines that only work if nothing goes wrong. We will say what we think is achievable and where the slack has to be.
