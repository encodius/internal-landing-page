What we do

Build, run, assure.

Three kinds of work. Most engagements draw on more than one, and we would rather tell you which you actually need than sell you all three.

Build

Getting the thing made - new products from first prototype to production, and older systems moved forward without stopping everything else.

Product engineering

Web and mobile products built end to end, from first prototype to production.

Legacy modernisation

Moving systems off ageing stacks incrementally, without a rewrite that stops everything else.

Run

Keeping it up - the platforms, pipelines and data flows that decide whether software is reliable on a bad day, not just a good one.

Platform and infrastructure

The systems, pipelines and operational tooling that keep software reliable at scale.

Data and automation

Reporting, integrations and AI-assisted features with a human in the loop.

Assure

Proving it holds - the testing and security work that finds problems before your users, your auditors or an attacker do.

Quality assurance

Test strategy, automated suites and release checks that catch regressions before your users do.

Security and penetration testing

Penetration testing, code and infrastructure review, and remediation guidance mapped to the standards you report against.

How we fit

We work in the stack you already run.

Our engineers have delivered on the mainstream web, mobile, cloud and data platforms. We adopt your conventions, your pipelines and your review process rather than importing our own - if your team already has a way of doing things, we fit into it rather than around it.

Each engagement is staffed for the work in front of it. We bring in the specialists a project actually calls for instead of shaping the problem around whoever happens to be free, and we say so plainly when something falls outside what we should be taking on.

Engagement

Three ways to work with us.

The right shape depends on what is already in place - whether you need the whole thing delivered, more hands on a direction that is already set, or one role you cannot fill.

A whole project

We take the brief, shape it and deliver it end to end. One team accountable for the outcome, with scope broken down and reviewed as it moves.

Engineers in your team

Our engineers join your existing team and work to your board, your process and your standards. For when the direction is set and the constraint is capacity.

A single specialist

One role you need and cannot fill - a security reviewer, a platform engineer, a test lead. Scoped tightly, for as long as it is useful.
