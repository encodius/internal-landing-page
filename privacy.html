Who we are

This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking

We do not use cookies. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels, tag managers, session recording, or any form of profiling. We do not build visitor profiles and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

The one thing this site stores in your browser is your language choice, saved under the name "encodius-lang". It is written only if you actually use the EN / SR switch, it stays on your device, and it is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

Typefaces are served from our own servers rather than a third-party font service, so simply loading a page does not disclose your IP address to any outside company.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and — where your message concerns working together — taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
