01The problem

Fraud teams are working around their tools.

Cases live in inboxes

Alerts arrive by email, evidence sits in attachments, and the current state of a case is whatever the last reply says.

Deadlines are counted by hand

Legal windows are measured in hours. A spreadsheet does not wake anyone up when one is about to close.

Audit is rebuilt after the fact

When a regulator asks what happened, someone reconstructs the timeline from mail archives and memory.

02The product

What Correlis does

One record per case, held to the same deadlines and permissions your compliance function already works to.

01

Case intake and creation

A guided wizard captures payer, recipient, transactions and channel, with account validation as you type.

02

The case as one story

Money flow, evidence, notes and correspondence on a single scrolling record instead of a stack of tabs.

03

Deadline alerts

Every legal window becomes a countdown on the queue, escalating before it breaches rather than after.

04

Email from inside the case

Templated notifications to clients and counterpart banks, edited before sending and logged against the case.

05

Audit trail and activity log

Who changed what, when, and from where — retained for ten years and exportable on request.

06

Analytics and back office

Volumes, recovery rates, ageing and analyst load, plus user, role and configuration management.

Also included: saved queue views, bulk actions, command palette, CSV and PDF export, in-app support tickets, light and dark interface. Full product detail →

03How it works

Four steps, one record.

STEP 1

Intake

The case is opened with the parties, amounts and channel recorded once and validated.

STEP 2

Triage

The queue orders work by what breaches first, not by what arrived first.

STEP 3

Act

Holds requested, counterpart banks notified, evidence attached, status moved with a reason.

STEP 4

Close and report

Outcome recorded, report filed, and the whole trail preserved for the retention period.
