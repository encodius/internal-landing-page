Correlis

Fraud case management for regulated teams.

A case in Correlis carries its parties, money flow, evidence, correspondence, deadlines and full history in one record — for as long as your retention policy requires.

Request a demo Ask a question

Case queue — saved views, deadline countdowns, two-line rows so the description is readable without opening the case.

01Capabilities

Case work

Guided case creation with payer, recipient and transaction capture
Account number validation and bank recognition on entry
Internal and external case types with different obligations
Related cases linked by shared account, device or recipient
Notes, measures, documents and evidence attached to the record
Assignment and ownership across analyst, supervisor and legal

Deadlines and queue

Countdown per legal deadline, visible before the case is opened
Breach warnings on the queue and in notifications
Saved views: critical first, my cases, unassigned, all active
Configurable columns, filters and bulk actions
Command palette and keyboard-first navigation
Next best action surfaced on each case

Correspondence

Email templates per scenario, payer, recipient and counterpart bank
Edit and preview before sending, from inside the case
Every message logged on the case timeline
Case chat between analysts, supervisors and legal
Notifications for assignment, status change and approaching deadlines

Compliance and administration

Audit trail of every action, retained ten years
Activity log searchable by user, case, action and date
Roles and permissions defined per screen and per action
User management, password policy and access review
Analytics on volumes, ageing, recovery and analyst load
CSV and PDF export for reporting and regulator requests
02Deployment

Installed where your case data already lives.

On premise or private cloud

Runs inside your environment. No case data is processed on infrastructure you do not control.

Integrates with what you have

API-first, with connectors to core banking, transaction monitoring, mail and identity providers.

Configured to your procedure

Case types, deadlines, statuses, roles, templates and fields are set up to match your written procedure.

Correlis is in pilot. We are working with a small number of fraud teams before general availability, which means the roadmap is still shaped by the first customers.
